# 终极稠密重建优化方案

本文档详细描述一个用时间换质量的极致稠密重建方案，融合PatchMatch、多轮几何一致性、因子图优化和概率融合。

---

## 数学建模基础

### 1. 问题定义

给定N个已标定的图像 $\{I_i\}_{i=1}^N$ 和对应的相机参数 $\{K_i, R_i, t_i\}$，目标是估计每个像素的：
- 深度值 $d \in [d_{min}, d_{max}]$
- 表面法向量 $\mathbf{n} \in \mathbb{S}^2$（单位球面）

### 2. 概率图模型

我们将稠密重建建模为**最大后验概率(MAP)估计**问题：

$$
\{\mathbf{d}^*, \mathbf{n}^*\} = \arg\max_{\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n}} P(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n} | \{I_i\})
$$

根据贝叶斯定理：

$$
P(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n} | \{I_i\}) \propto P(\{I_i\} | \mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n}) \cdot P(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n})
$$

取负对数，转换为**能量最小化**：

$$
E(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n}) = E_{data}(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n}) + E_{prior}(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n})
$$

### 3. 因子图表示

将能量函数分解为因子图：

$$
E(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n}) = \sum_{p} \phi_p^{photo}(d_p, \mathbf{n}_p) + \sum_{p,q} \phi_{pq}^{geom}(d_p, d_q) + \sum_{p,q \in \mathcal{N}} \phi_{pq}^{smooth}(d_p, d_q) + \sum_p \phi_p^{prior}(d_p, \mathbf{n}_p)
$$

其中：
- $\phi^{photo}$: 光度一致性因子
- $\phi^{geom}$: 几何一致性因子
- $\phi^{smooth}$: 平滑性因子
- $\phi^{prior}$: 先验因子
- $\mathcal{N}$: 像素邻域关系

## 因子详细数学建模

### 因子1: 光度一致性 (Photometric Factor)

#### 1.1 单应性变换

对于参考视图像素 $\mathbf{p} = (u, v)^T$，假设深度为 $d$，法向量为 $\mathbf{n}$，到源视图的单应矩阵为：

$$
H_{ref \to src} = K_{src} \left( R_{rel} - \frac{\mathbf{t}_{rel} \mathbf{n}^T}{d} \right) K_{ref}^{-1}
$$

其中：
- $R_{rel} = R_{src} R_{ref}^T$: 相对旋转
- $\mathbf{t}_{rel} = \mathbf{t}_{src} - R_{rel} \mathbf{t}_{ref}$: 相对平移

#### 1.2 双边加权NCC

$$
NCC(\mathbf{p}, d, \mathbf{n}) = \frac{\sum_{i \in \Omega} w_i (I_{ref}(\mathbf{p}_i) - \bar{I}_{ref}) (I_{src}(\mathbf{p}'_i) - \bar{I}_{src})}{\sqrt{\sum_i w_i (I_{ref}(\mathbf{p}_i) - \bar{I}_{ref})^2} \sqrt{\sum_i w_i (I_{src}(\mathbf{p}'_i) - \bar{I}_{src})^2}}
$$

双边权重：
$$
w_i = \exp\left(-\frac{\|\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}\|^2}{2\sigma_s^2}\right) \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{(I_{ref}(\mathbf{p}_i) - I_{ref}(\mathbf{p}))^2}{2\sigma_c^2}\right)
$$

加权均值：
$$
\bar{I}_{ref} = \frac{\sum_i w_i I_{ref}(\mathbf{p}_i)}{\sum_i w_i}, \quad \bar{I}_{src} = \frac{\sum_i w_i I_{src}(\mathbf{p}'_i)}{\sum_i w_i}
$$

#### 1.3 多尺度多特征融合

$$
C_{photo}(\mathbf{p}, d, \mathbf{n}) = \sum_{s \in \{7,15,31\}} \alpha_s \left[ \beta_1 C_{NCC}^s + \beta_2 C_{Census}^s + \beta_3 C_{Grad}^s + \beta_4 C_{Color}^s \right]
$$

自适应权重：
$$
\beta_1 = \frac{0.4 (1 + \tau(\mathbf{p}))}{\sum \beta}, \quad \beta_2 = \frac{0.3 (1 + \epsilon(\mathbf{p}))}{\sum \beta}
$$

其中 $\tau(\mathbf{p})$ 是纹理强度，$\epsilon(\mathbf{p})$ 是边缘强度。

#### 1.4 多视图聚合

假设各视图观测独立，似然函数：
$$
P(\{I_i\} | d, \mathbf{n}) = \prod_{i \in \mathcal{V}_{valid}} P(I_i | d, \mathbf{n})
$$

鲁棒聚合（截断均值）：
$$
C_{multi}(d, \mathbf{n}) = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{V}_{trunc}|} \sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}_{trunc}} w_i \cdot C_i(d, \mathbf{n})
$$

其中 $\mathcal{V}_{trunc}$ 是去掉最差30%后的视图集合。

#### 1.5 光度因子概率模型

假设匹配代价服从**截断高斯分布**：
$$
P(I | d, \mathbf{n}) \propto \exp\left(-\frac{\rho(C_{photo}(d, \mathbf{n}))}{2\sigma_{photo}^2}\right)
$$

使用Huber鲁棒核：
$$
\rho(x) = \begin{cases}
x^2 & |x| \leq \delta \\
2\delta|x| - \delta^2 & |x| > \delta
\end{cases}
$$

**光度因子能量**：
$$
\phi_p^{photo}(d_p, \mathbf{n}_p) = \rho_{Huber}\left(\frac{C_{multi}(d_p, \mathbf{n}_p)}{\sigma_{photo}}\right)
$$

### 因子2: 几何一致性 (Geometric Factor)

#### 2.1 重投影误差模型

对于参考视图像素 $\mathbf{p}_{ref}$，深度 $d_{ref}$：

**前向投影**：
$$
\mathbf{X} = d_{ref} \cdot K_{ref}^{-1} \tilde{\mathbf{p}}_{ref}
$$
$$
\mathbf{p}_{src} = \pi(K_{src}(R_{src}\mathbf{X} + \mathbf{t}_{src}))
$$

**后向投影**：
$$
\mathbf{X}' = d_{src} \cdot K_{src}^{-1} \tilde{\mathbf{p}}_{src}
$$
$$
\mathbf{p}'_{ref} = \pi(K_{ref}(R_{ref}\mathbf{X}' + \mathbf{t}_{ref}))
$$

**重投影误差**：
$$
e_{reproj} = \|\mathbf{p}_{ref} - \mathbf{p}'_{ref}\|_2
$$

#### 2.2 深度一致性误差

期望深度 vs 实际深度：
$$
d_{src}^{expected} = \mathbf{e}_3^T (R_{src}\mathbf{X} + \mathbf{t}_{src})
$$

深度相对误差：
$$
e_{depth} = \frac{|d_{src} - d_{src}^{expected}|}{d_{src}^{expected}}
$$

#### 2.3 法向量一致性

将法向量转换到世界坐标系：
$$
\mathbf{n}_{ref}^{world} = R_{ref}^T \mathbf{n}_{ref}, \quad \mathbf{n}_{src}^{world} = R_{src}^T \mathbf{n}_{src}
$$

法向量角度误差：
$$
e_{normal} = \arccos(\mathbf{n}_{ref}^{world} \cdot \mathbf{n}_{src}^{world})
$$

#### 2.4 几何因子概率模型

假设几何误差服从**混合高斯分布**（考虑遮挡outlier）：
$$
P(consistent | d_{ref}, d_{src}) = (1-\pi_{occ}) \cdot \mathcal{N}(e; 0, \Sigma_{geom}) + \pi_{occ} \cdot Uniform
$$

**几何因子能量**：
$$
\phi_{pq}^{geom}(d_p, d_q) = \lambda_{geom} \left[ \rho_{Huber}\left(\frac{e_{reproj}}{\sigma_r}\right) + \gamma_d \rho_{Huber}\left(\frac{e_{depth}}{\sigma_d}\right) + \gamma_n \rho_{Huber}\left(\frac{e_{normal}}{\sigma_n}\right) \right]
$$

参数设置：
- $\sigma_r = 1.0$ 像素
- $\sigma_d = 0.01$ (1%)
- $\sigma_n = 0.26$ rad (15°)
- $\gamma_d = 10, \gamma_n = 5$

---

### 因子3: 平滑性 (Smoothness Factor)

#### 3.1 边缘感知平滑

对于相邻像素 $p, q \in \mathcal{N}$：

**深度梯度**：
$$
\nabla d_{pq} = \frac{|d_p - d_q|}{\max(d_p, d_q)}
$$

**边缘感知权重**（基于颜色梯度）：
$$
w_{pq} = \exp\left(-\frac{|I_p - I_q|^2}{2\sigma_{edge}^2}\right)
$$

#### 3.2 平滑因子概率模型

假设深度梯度服从**截断Laplace分布**：
$$
P(d_p, d_q) \propto \exp\left(-\frac{w_{pq} \cdot |\nabla d_{pq}|}{\sigma_{smooth}}\right)
$$

**平滑因子能量**：
$$
\phi_{pq}^{smooth}(d_p, d_q) = \lambda_{smooth} \cdot w_{pq} \cdot \rho_{Huber}\left(\frac{\nabla d_{pq}}{\sigma_{smooth}}\right)
$$

#### 3.3 各向异性扩展

考虑法向量的平滑：
$$
\phi_{pq}^{smooth}(d_p, d_q, \mathbf{n}_p, \mathbf{n}_q) = \lambda_d \cdot w_{pq} \cdot \rho(\nabla d_{pq}) + \lambda_n \cdot w_{pq} \cdot \rho(1 - \mathbf{n}_p \cdot \mathbf{n}_q)
$$

---

### 因子4: 先验 (Prior Factor)

#### 4.1 Delaunay平面先验

对于稀疏点构成的Delaunay三角形内的像素：

**平面方程**：
$$
\mathbf{n}_{prior} \cdot \mathbf{X} + d_{prior} = 0
$$

**先验深度**：
$$
d_{prior}(\mathbf{p}) = -\frac{d_{prior}}{\mathbf{n}_{prior} \cdot K^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf{p}}}
$$

#### 4.2 先验因子概率模型

$$
P(d | d_{prior}) = \mathcal{N}(d; d_{prior}, \sigma_{prior}^2)
$$

**自适应置信度**（基于纹理）：
$$
\sigma_{prior}(\mathbf{p}) = \sigma_0 \cdot (1 + \alpha \cdot \tau(\mathbf{p}))
$$

弱纹理区域 $\tau$ 小，$\sigma_{prior}$ 小，先验权重大。

**先验因子能量**：
$$
\phi_p^{prior}(d_p, \mathbf{n}_p) = \lambda_{prior} \left[ \frac{(d_p - d_{prior})^2}{2\sigma_{prior}^2} + \frac{(1 - \mathbf{n}_p \cdot \mathbf{n}_{prior})^2}{2\sigma_{n,prior}^2} \right]
$$

---

### 4.3 多假设先验

保留的K个假设形成混合高斯先验：
$$
P(d) = \sum_{k=1}^K \pi_k \cdot \mathcal{N}(d; d_k, \sigma_k^2)
$$

其中 $\pi_k$ 由PatchMatch阶段的代价确定：
$$
\pi_k = \frac{\exp(-C_k / T)}{\sum_j \exp(-C_j / T)}
$$

---

## 完整能量函数

$$
\boxed{
E(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{n}) = \sum_{p} \underbrace{\rho_H\left(\frac{C_{multi}(d_p, \mathbf{n}_p)}{\sigma_{photo}}\right)}_{光度项}
+ \lambda_g \sum_{p,q} \underbrace{\rho_H\left(\frac{e_{geom}(d_p, d_q)}{\sigma_{geom}}\right)}_{几何项}
+ \lambda_s \sum_{p,q \in \mathcal{N}} \underbrace{w_{pq} \cdot \rho_H(\nabla d_{pq})}_{平滑项}
+ \lambda_p \sum_p \underbrace{\frac{(d_p - d_{prior})^2}{\sigma_{prior}^2}}_{先验项}
}
$$

## 优化求解

### 1. 高斯-牛顿法

对于因子 $\phi_i(\mathbf{x})$，线性化：
$$
\phi_i(\mathbf{x} + \Delta\mathbf{x}) \approx \phi_i(\mathbf{x}) + J_i \Delta\mathbf{x}
$$

正规方程：
$$
(J^T \Sigma^{-1} J) \Delta\mathbf{x} = -J^T \Sigma^{-1} \mathbf{r}
$$

其中 $\mathbf{r}$ 是残差向量，$\Sigma$ 是噪声协方差。

### 2. 稀疏结构

因子图的Hessian矩阵 $H = J^T \Sigma^{-1} J$ 是**稀疏**的：
- 光度因子：对角块
- 几何因子：跨视图连接
- 平滑因子：相邻像素连接

使用**稀疏Cholesky分解**求解。

### 3. 鲁棒核的IRLS

对于Huber核，使用**迭代重加权最小二乘(IRLS)**：

$$
w_i^{(k)} = \frac{\rho'(r_i^{(k)})}{r_i^{(k)}}
$$

等价于加权最小二乘：
$$
\min_{\Delta\mathbf{x}} \sum_i w_i^{(k)} \|r_i + J_i \Delta\mathbf{x}\|^2
$$

### 4. 不确定性估计

优化后的协方差矩阵：
$$
\Sigma_{\mathbf{d}} = (J^T \Sigma^{-1} J)^{-1}
$$

每个像素的深度不确定性：
$$
\sigma_{d_p} = \sqrt{[\Sigma_{\mathbf{d}}]_{pp}}
$$

**流程**:
```cpp
void MultiResolutionPatchMatch(DepthMap& depth, const ImagePyramid& pyramid) {
    vector<DepthMap> depth_pyramid(num_levels);

    for (int round = 0; round < 3; round++) {
        // ===== 粗到细 =====
        for (int level = num_levels - 1; level >= 0; level--) {
            if (level == num_levels - 1 && round == 0) {
                // 最粗层首轮: 随机初始化 + Delaunay先验
                RandomInitWithPrior(depth_pyramid[level]);
            } else if (level < num_levels - 1) {
                // 上采样: JBU边缘保持
                depth_pyramid[level] = JBU_Upsample(
                    depth_pyramid[level + 1],
                    pyramid.images[level],
                    sigma_spatial, sigma_color
                );
            }

            // 该层PatchMatch优化
            PatchMatchOptimize(depth_pyramid[level], pyramid.images[level],
                               iterations_per_level[level]);
        }

        // ===== 细到粗 (传播精细层信息) =====
        for (int level = 0; level < num_levels - 1; level++) {
            DepthMap downsampled = Downsample(depth_pyramid[level]);
            depth_pyramid[level + 1] = MergeDepthMaps(
                depth_pyramid[level + 1], downsampled
            );
            PatchMatchOptimize(depth_pyramid[level + 1], pyramid.images[level + 1],
                               iterations_per_level[level + 1] / 2);
        }
    }

    depth = depth_pyramid[0];
}
```

**JBU上采样实现**:
```cpp
DepthMap JBU_Upsample(const DepthMap& low_depth, const Image& high_image,
                      float sigma_s, float sigma_c) {
    int H = high_image.rows, W = high_image.cols;
    float scale = (float)low_depth.cols / W;
    DepthMap result(H, W);

    int win_size = (int)(1.0f / scale) + 1;
    int half_win = win_size / 2;

    for (int v = 0; v < H; v++) {
        for (int u = 0; u < W; u++) {
            float o_x = u * scale, o_y = v * scale;
            float ref_color = high_image(v, u);

            float depth_sum = 0, weight_sum = 0;

            for (int dy = -half_win; dy <= half_win; dy++) {
                for (int dx = -half_win; dx <= half_win; dx++) {
                    int lx = clamp((int)(o_x + dx), 0, low_depth.cols - 1);
                    int ly = clamp((int)(o_y + dy), 0, low_depth.rows - 1);

                    float src_depth = low_depth(ly, lx);
                    if (src_depth <= 0) continue;

                    // 高分辨率图像对应位置的颜色
                    int hx = clamp(u + dx, 0, W - 1);
                    int hy = clamp(v + dy, 0, H - 1);
                    float neighbor_color = high_image(hy, hx);

                    // 双边权重
                    float spatial_w = exp(-(dx*dx + dy*dy) / (2 * sigma_s * sigma_s));
                    float color_w = exp(-pow(ref_color - neighbor_color, 2) / (2 * sigma_c * sigma_c));
                    float weight = spatial_w * color_w;

                    depth_sum += weight * src_depth;
                    weight_sum += weight;
                }
            }

            result(v, u) = (weight_sum > 0) ? depth_sum / weight_sum : 0;
        }
    }
    return result;
}
```

```cpp
void SuperDensePropagation(int u, int v, DepthMap& depth, NormalMap& normal,
                           ConfidenceMap& conf, const Image& img) {
    vector<Hypothesis> candidates;

    // ===== 1. 近邻传播 (24个) =====
    for (int dy = -near_radius; dy <= near_radius; dy++) {
        for (int dx = -near_radius; dx <= near_radius; dx++) {
            if (dx == 0 && dy == 0) continue;
            if (abs(dx) + abs(dy) > near_radius) continue;  // 菱形区域

            int nx = u + dx, ny = v + dy;
            if (!IsValid(nx, ny)) continue;

            if (depth(ny, nx) > 0 && conf(ny, nx) < conf(v, u)) {
                // 插值深度到当前位置
                float d = InterpolateDepth(nx, ny, u, v, depth(ny, nx), normal(ny, nx));
                candidates.push_back({d, normal(ny, nx), conf(ny, nx)});
            }
        }
    }

    // ===== 2. 远邻传播 (24个方向, 自适应搜索) =====
    float angles[24];  // 24个均匀分布的方向
    for (int i = 0; i < 24; i++) angles[i] = i * M_PI / 12;

    for (int i = 0; i < 24; i++) {
        float best_cost = FLT_MAX;
        int best_x = -1, best_y = -1;

        // 在该方向上搜索最小代价点
        for (int dist = far_min_dist; dist <= far_max_dist; dist += far_step) {
            int nx = u + (int)(dist * cos(angles[i]));
            int ny = v + (int)(dist * sin(angles[i]));

            if (!IsValid(nx, ny) || depth(ny, nx) <= 0) continue;

            if (conf(ny, nx) < best_cost) {
                best_cost = conf(ny, nx);
                best_x = nx;
                best_y = ny;
            }
        }

        if (best_x >= 0) {
            float d = InterpolateDepth(best_x, best_y, u, v,
                                       depth(best_y, best_x), normal(best_y, best_x));
            candidates.push_back({d, normal(best_y, best_x), best_cost});
        }
    }

    // ===== 3. 全局相似patch搜索 =====
    if (enable_global_search) {
        // 计算当前patch的描述子
        Descriptor desc = ComputePatchDescriptor(img, u, v);

        // 在预建的KD树中搜索最相似的K个patch
        vector<Match> matches = kd_tree.KNNSearch(desc, global_search_k);

        for (auto& m : matches) {
            if (depth(m.y, m.x) > 0) {
                float d = InterpolateDepth(m.x, m.y, u, v,
                                          depth(m.y, m.x), normal(m.y, m.x));
                candidates.push_back({d, normal(m.y, m.x), conf(m.y, m.x) + 0.1f});
            }
        }
    }

    // ===== 4. 评估所有候选假设 =====
    for (auto& cand : candidates) {
        float cost = ComputeMultiScaleMultiFeatureCost(u, v, cand.depth, cand.normal);
        if (cost < conf(v, u)) {
            depth(v, u) = cand.depth;
            normal(v, u) = cand.normal;
            conf(v, u) = cost;
        }
    }
}
```

```cpp
vector<Hypothesis> DenseHypothesisGeneration(int u, int v, const Camera& cam,
                                              float d_min, float d_max) {
    vector<Hypothesis> all_hypotheses;

    // ===== 1. 粗采样: 100深度 × 50法向量 =====
    // 逆深度均匀采样 (远近都能覆盖)
    vector<float> depth_samples(num_depth_samples);
    float inv_d_min = 1.0f / d_max, inv_d_max = 1.0f / d_min;
    for (int i = 0; i < num_depth_samples; i++) {
        float inv_d = inv_d_min + i * (inv_d_max - inv_d_min) / (num_depth_samples - 1);
        depth_samples[i] = 1.0f / inv_d;
    }

    // 法向量均匀球面采样 (Fibonacci lattice)
    vector<Normal> normal_samples(num_normal_samples);
    float golden_ratio = (1 + sqrt(5)) / 2;
    for (int i = 0; i < num_normal_samples; i++) {
        float theta = 2 * M_PI * i / golden_ratio;
        float phi = acos(1 - 2 * (i + 0.5f) / num_normal_samples);

        Normal n(sin(phi) * cos(theta), sin(phi) * sin(theta), cos(phi));

        // 确保法向量朝向相机
        Point3 view_dir = cam.ViewDirection(u, v);
        if (n.dot(view_dir) > 0) n = -n;

        normal_samples[i] = n;
    }

    // 评估所有组合
    for (float d : depth_samples) {
        for (const Normal& n : normal_samples) {
            float cost = ComputeCost(u, v, d, n);
            all_hypotheses.push_back({d, n, cost});
        }
    }

    // ===== 2. 选择top-10进行精细搜索 =====
    sort(all_hypotheses.begin(), all_hypotheses.end(),
         [](const Hypothesis& a, const Hypothesis& b) { return a.cost < b.cost; });

    vector<Hypothesis> refined;
    for (int i = 0; i < min(top_k_refine, (int)all_hypotheses.size()); i++) {
        Hypothesis best = all_hypotheses[i];

        // 精细搜索
        float range = 1.0f;
        for (int iter = 0; iter < refine_iterations; iter++) {
            // 深度扰动
            float d_new = best.depth * (1 + (rand01() - 0.5f) * refine_depth_range * range);
            d_new = clamp(d_new, d_min, d_max);

            // 法向量扰动
            Normal n_new = PerturbNormal(best.normal, refine_normal_range * range);

            float cost_new = ComputeCost(u, v, d_new, n_new);
            if (cost_new < best.cost) {
                best = {d_new, n_new, cost_new};
            }

            range *= 0.8f;  // 缩小搜索范围
        }

        refined.push_back(best);
    }

    // ===== 3. 保留top-5假设 =====
    sort(refined.begin(), refined.end(),
         [](const Hypothesis& a, const Hypothesis& b) { return a.cost < b.cost; });

    vector<Hypothesis> final_hypotheses;
    for (int i = 0; i < min(num_hypotheses_keep, (int)refined.size()); i++) {
        final_hypotheses.push_back(refined[i]);
    }

    // 计算概率分布 (softmax)
    float sum_exp = 0;
    for (auto& h : final_hypotheses) {
        h.probability = exp(-h.cost * 10);  // temperature = 0.1
        sum_exp += h.probability;
    }
    for (auto& h : final_hypotheses) {
        h.probability /= sum_exp;
    }

    return final_hypotheses;
}
```

```cpp
float ComputeMultiScaleMultiFeatureCost(int u, int v, float depth, const Normal& normal,
                                         const Image& ref, const vector<Image>& srcs,
                                         const Camera& ref_cam, const vector<Camera>& src_cams) {
    // 计算自适应权重
    float texture = ComputeTextureStrength(ref, u, v);
    float edge = ComputeEdgeStrength(ref, u, v);

    float w_ncc = base_weight_ncc * (1 + texture);
    float w_census = base_weight_census * (1 + edge);
    float w_gradient = base_weight_gradient;
    float w_color = base_weight_color;
    float w_sum = w_ncc + w_census + w_gradient + w_color;
    w_ncc /= w_sum; w_census /= w_sum; w_gradient /= w_sum; w_color /= w_sum;

    float total_cost = 0;

    // 对每个源视图
    for (int s = 0; s < srcs.size(); s++) {
        // 计算单应矩阵
        Matrix3f H = ComputeHomography(ref_cam, src_cams[s], depth, normal);

        // 检查投影是否有效
        Point2f center_proj = WarpPoint(H, Point2f(u, v));
        if (!IsInsideImage(center_proj, srcs[s])) continue;

        float view_cost = 0;

        // ===== 多尺度 =====
        for (int ps = 0; ps < patch_sizes.size(); ps++) {
            int patch_size = patch_sizes[ps];
            float patch_weight = patch_weights[ps];

            float feature_cost = 0;

            // ===== 多特征 =====
            if (use_bilateral_ncc) {
                float ncc = ComputeBilateralNCC(ref, srcs[s], H, u, v, patch_size);
                feature_cost += w_ncc * (1 - ncc);
            }

            if (use_census) {
                float census = ComputeCensusCost(ref, srcs[s], H, u, v, patch_size);
                feature_cost += w_census * census / (patch_size * patch_size);
            }

            if (use_gradient) {
                float grad = ComputeGradientCost(ref, srcs[s], H, u, v, patch_size);
                feature_cost += w_gradient * grad;
            }

            if (use_color_ncc) {
                float color = ComputeColorNCC_Lab(ref, srcs[s], H, u, v, patch_size);
                feature_cost += w_color * (1 - color);
            }

            view_cost += patch_weight * feature_cost;
        }

        total_cost += view_cost;
    }

    return total_cost / srcs.size();
}

// 双边NCC
float ComputeBilateralNCC(const Image& ref, const Image& src, const Matrix3f& H,
                          int cx, int cy, int patch_size) {
    int half = patch_size / 2;
    float center_color = ref(cy, cx);

    float sum_ref = 0, sum_src = 0;
    float sum_ref2 = 0, sum_src2 = 0, sum_ref_src = 0;
    float weight_sum = 0;

    for (int dy = -half; dy <= half; dy++) {
        for (int dx = -half; dx <= half; dx++) {
            int rx = cx + dx, ry = cy + dy;
            Point2f sp = WarpPoint(H, Point2f(rx, ry));

            if (!IsInsideImage(sp, src)) continue;

            float ref_val = ref(ry, rx);
            float src_val = BilinearSample(src, sp);

            // 双边权重
            float spatial_w = exp(-(dx*dx + dy*dy) / (2 * sigma_s * sigma_s));
            float color_w = exp(-pow(ref_val - center_color, 2) / (2 * sigma_c * sigma_c));
            float w = spatial_w * color_w;

            sum_ref += w * ref_val;
            sum_src += w * src_val;
            sum_ref2 += w * ref_val * ref_val;
            sum_src2 += w * src_val * src_val;
            sum_ref_src += w * ref_val * src_val;
            weight_sum += w;
        }
    }

    if (weight_sum < 1e-6) return 0;

    sum_ref /= weight_sum;
    sum_src /= weight_sum;
    sum_ref2 /= weight_sum;
    sum_src2 /= weight_sum;
    sum_ref_src /= weight_sum;

    float var_ref = sum_ref2 - sum_ref * sum_ref;
    float var_src = sum_src2 - sum_src * sum_src;

    if (var_ref < 1e-6 || var_src < 1e-6) return 0;

    float covar = sum_ref_src - sum_ref * sum_src;
    return covar / sqrt(var_ref * var_src);
}

// Census代价
float ComputeCensusCost(const Image& ref, const Image& src, const Matrix3f& H,
                        int cx, int cy, int patch_size) {
    int half = patch_size / 2;
    int hamming_sum = 0;
    int count = 0;

    float ref_center = ref(cy, cx);
    Point2f src_center = WarpPoint(H, Point2f(cx, cy));
    float src_center_val = BilinearSample(src, src_center);

    for (int dy = -half; dy <= half; dy++) {
        for (int dx = -half; dx <= half; dx++) {
            if (dx == 0 && dy == 0) continue;

            int rx = cx + dx, ry = cy + dy;
            Point2f sp = WarpPoint(H, Point2f(rx, ry));

            if (!IsInsideImage(sp, src)) continue;

            float ref_val = ref(ry, rx);
            float src_val = BilinearSample(src, sp);

            // Census比较
            int ref_bit = (ref_val < ref_center) ? 1 : 0;
            int src_bit = (src_val < src_center_val) ? 1 : 0;

            hamming_sum += (ref_bit != src_bit) ? 1 : 0;
            count++;
        }
    }

    return (count > 0) ? (float)hamming_sum / count : 1.0f;
}
```

```cpp
struct ViewAggregationConfig {
    bool use_all_views = true;
    float truncate_ratio = 0.3f;         // 去掉最差30%
    float min_valid_ratio = 0.3f;        // 至少30%视图有效

    // 视图权重因素
    float baseline_angle_min = 5.0f;     // 度
    float baseline_angle_max = 30.0f;
    float baseline_angle_optimal = 12.0f;
};

float AggregateMultiViewCost(int u, int v, float depth, const Normal& normal,
                              const Image& ref, const vector<Image>& srcs,
                              const Camera& ref_cam, const vector<Camera>& src_cams) {
    vector<pair<float, float>> costs_weights;  // (cost, weight)

    for (int s = 0; s < srcs.size(); s++) {
        // 计算基线角度
        float angle = ComputeBaselineAngle(ref_cam, src_cams[s], u, v, depth);
        if (angle < baseline_angle_min || angle > baseline_angle_max) continue;

        // 视图权重 (基于角度)
        float angle_weight = 1.0f - abs(angle - baseline_angle_optimal) / baseline_angle_max;

        // 遮挡检测权重
        float occlusion_weight = EstimateVisibility(ref_cam, src_cams[s], u, v, depth);

        float weight = angle_weight * occlusion_weight;
        if (weight < 0.1f) continue;

        // 计算该视图的匹配代价
        float cost = ComputeMultiScaleMultiFeatureCost(u, v, depth, normal,
                                                        ref, {srcs[s]},
                                                        ref_cam, {src_cams[s]});

        costs_weights.push_back({cost, weight});
    }

    if (costs_weights.size() < srcs.size() * min_valid_ratio) {
        return 2.0f;  // 无效
    }

    // 按代价排序
    sort(costs_weights.begin(), costs_weights.end());

    // 截断均值 (去掉最差30%)
    int keep_count = (int)(costs_weights.size() * (1 - truncate_ratio));
    keep_count = max(keep_count, 2);

    float cost_sum = 0, weight_sum = 0;
    for (int i = 0; i < keep_count; i++) {
        cost_sum += costs_weights[i].first * costs_weights[i].second;
        weight_sum += costs_weights[i].second;
    }

    return cost_sum / weight_sum;
}
```

```cpp
void PatchMatchOptimize(DepthMap& depth, NormalMap& normal, ConfidenceMap& conf,
                        const Image& img, int max_iterations) {
    float prev_avg_cost = FLT_MAX;

    for (int iter = 0; iter < max_iterations; iter++) {
        float total_cost = 0;
        int count = 0;

        // 棋盘格传播
        bool red_phase = (iter % 2 == 0);

        #pragma omp parallel for reduction(+:total_cost, count)
        for (int v = 0; v < H; v++) {
            for (int u = 0; u < W; u++) {
                if (((u + v) % 2 == 0) != red_phase) continue;
                if (depth(v, u) <= 0) continue;

                // 1. 邻域传播
                SuperDensePropagation(u, v, depth, normal, conf, img);

                // 2. 随机搜索/精细优化
                RandomRefinement(u, v, depth, normal, conf);

                total_cost += conf(v, u);
                count++;
            }
        }

        // 收敛检测
        float avg_cost = total_cost / count;
        float change = abs(prev_avg_cost - avg_cost) / prev_avg_cost;

        if (iter >= min_iterations && change < convergence_threshold) {
            printf("Converged at iteration %d, change = %.4f%%\n", iter, change * 100);
            break;
        }

        prev_avg_cost = avg_cost;
    }
}
```

```cpp
void MultiRoundGeometricConsistency(vector<DepthMap>& depths,
                                     vector<NormalMap>& normals,
                                     vector<ConfidenceMap>& confs,
                                     const vector<Image>& images,
                                     const vector<Camera>& cameras) {
    for (int round = 0; round < num_rounds; round++) {
        printf("Geometric consistency round %d/%d\n", round + 1, num_rounds);

        // 对每个参考视图
        for (int ref = 0; ref < images.size(); ref++) {
            int inconsistent_count = 0;

            #pragma omp parallel for reduction(+:inconsistent_count)
            for (int v = 0; v < H; v++) {
                for (int u = 0; u < W; u++) {
                    if (depths[ref](v, u) <= 0) continue;

                    // ===== 2.1 跨视图深度传播 =====
                    vector<Hypothesis> cross_view_hypotheses;

                    for (int src = 0; src < images.size(); src++) {
                        if (src == ref) continue;

                        // 从源视图获取投影假设
                        Point3 X = cameras[ref].Unproject(u, v, depths[ref](v, u));
                        Point2 p_src = cameras[src].Project(X);

                        if (IsInsideImage(p_src, images[src]) && depths[src](p_src) > 0) {
                            // 将源视图的深度投影回参考视图
                            Point3 X_src = cameras[src].Unproject(p_src, depths[src](p_src));
                            Point2 p_ref_back = cameras[ref].Project(X_src);
                            float d_ref_from_src = cameras[ref].Depth(X_src);

                            cross_view_hypotheses.push_back({
                                d_ref_from_src,
                                normals[src](p_src),
                                confs[src](p_src)
                            });
                        }
                    }

                    // ===== 2.2 双向一致性检查 =====
                    int consistent_views = 0;

                    for (int src = 0; src < images.size(); src++) {
                        if (src == ref) continue;

                        if (CheckBidirectionalConsistency(
                                depths[ref], depths[src], normals[ref], normals[src],
                                cameras[ref], cameras[src], u, v)) {
                            consistent_views++;
                        }
                    }

                    // ===== 2.3 不一致区域重估计 =====
                    if (consistent_views < 2) {
                        inconsistent_count++;

                        // 尝试跨视图假设
                        bool improved = false;
                        for (auto& hyp : cross_view_hypotheses) {
                            float cost = ComputeMultiScaleMultiFeatureCost(
                                u, v, hyp.depth, hyp.normal, ...);
                            if (cost < confs[ref](v, u)) {
                                depths[ref](v, u) = hyp.depth;
                                normals[ref](v, u) = hyp.normal;
                                confs[ref](v, u) = cost;
                                improved = true;
                            }
                        }

                        // 局部PatchMatch重估计
                        if (!improved) {
                            LocalPatchMatchRefine(u, v, depths[ref], normals[ref],
                                                  confs[ref], images[ref],
                                                  local_refine_iterations);
                        }
                    }
                }
            }

            printf("  View %d: %d inconsistent pixels\n", ref, inconsistent_count);
        }
    }
}

bool CheckBidirectionalConsistency(const DepthMap& depth_ref, const DepthMap& depth_src,
                                    const NormalMap& normal_ref, const NormalMap& normal_src,
                                    const Camera& cam_ref, const Camera& cam_src,
                                    int u, int v) {
    float d_ref = depth_ref(v, u);
    Normal n_ref = normal_ref(v, u);

    // 前向: ref → src
    Point3 X = cam_ref.Unproject(u, v, d_ref);
    Point2 p_src = cam_src.Project(X);

    if (!IsInsideImage(p_src, depth_src)) return false;

    float d_src = depth_src(p_src);
    if (d_src <= 0) return false;

    float d_src_expected = cam_src.Depth(X);
    if (abs(d_src - d_src_expected) / d_src_expected > depth_threshold) return false;

    // 后向: src → ref
    Point3 X_src = cam_src.Unproject(p_src, d_src);
    Point2 p_ref_back = cam_ref.Project(X_src);

    float reproj_error = sqrt(pow(u - p_ref_back.x, 2) + pow(v - p_ref_back.y, 2));
    if (reproj_error > reproj_threshold) return false;

    // 法向量一致性
    Normal n_src = normal_src(p_src);
    // 转换到世界坐标系
    Normal n_ref_world = cam_ref.R.transpose() * n_ref;
    Normal n_src_world = cam_src.R.transpose() * n_src;

    float angle = acos(clamp(n_ref_world.dot(n_src_world), -1.0f, 1.0f)) * 180 / M_PI;
    if (angle > normal_threshold) return false;

    return true;
}
```

```cpp
void FactorGraphOptimization(vector<DepthMap>& depths,
                              vector<NormalMap>& normals,
                              vector<ConfidenceMap>& confs,
                              const vector<Image>& images,
                              const vector<Camera>& cameras,
                              const vector<DepthMap>& prior_depths,
                              const FactorGraphConfig& config) {

    // 对每个分辨率层级
    for (int level = num_levels - 1; level >= 0; level--) {
        printf("Factor graph optimization level %d\n", level);

        int scale = 1 << level;
        int H_level = H / scale, W_level = W / scale;

        // 为每个视图构建因子图
        for (int ref = 0; ref < images.size(); ref++) {
            ceres::Problem problem;

            // 深度变量数组
            vector<double> depth_vars(H_level * W_level);
            for (int i = 0; i < depth_vars.size(); i++) {
                depth_vars[i] = depths[ref].at_level(level, i);
            }

            for (int v = 0; v < H_level; v++) {
                for (int u = 0; u < W_level; u++) {
                    int idx = v * W_level + u;

                    if (depth_vars[idx] <= 0) continue;

                    // ===== 光度因子 =====
                    ceres::CostFunction* photo_factor = new PhotometricFactor(
                        images[ref], GetOtherImages(images, ref),
                        cameras[ref], GetOtherCameras(cameras, ref),
                        u * scale, v * scale, normals[ref](v, u), config);

                    problem.AddResidualBlock(
                        photo_factor,
                        new ceres::HuberLoss(config.huber_delta_photo),
                        &depth_vars[idx]);

                    // ===== 几何因子 (与每个源视图) =====
                    for (int src = 0; src < images.size(); src++) {
                        if (src == ref) continue;

                        Point3 X = cameras[ref].Unproject(u * scale, v * scale, depth_vars[idx]);
                        Point2 p_src = cameras[src].Project(X);
                        int u_src = p_src.x / scale, v_src = p_src.y / scale;

                        if (u_src >= 0 && u_src < W_level && v_src >= 0 && v_src < H_level) {
                            int idx_src = v_src * W_level + u_src;
                            if (depths[src].at_level(level, idx_src) > 0) {
                                ceres::CostFunction* geom_factor = new GeometricFactor(
                                    cameras[ref], cameras[src],
                                    u * scale, v * scale, config.weight_geometric);

                                problem.AddResidualBlock(
                                    geom_factor,
                                    new ceres::HuberLoss(config.huber_delta_geom),
                                    &depth_vars[idx],
                                    &depth_vars_src[src][idx_src]);
                            }
                        }
                    }

                    // ===== 平滑因子 (4邻域) =====
                    int neighbors[4] = {idx - 1, idx + 1, idx - W_level, idx + W_level};
                    int neighbor_u[4] = {u - 1, u + 1, u, u};
                    int neighbor_v[4] = {v, v, v - 1, v + 1};

                    for (int n = 0; n < 4; n++) {
                        int nu = neighbor_u[n], nv = neighbor_v[n];
                        if (nu < 0 || nu >= W_level || nv < 0 || nv >= H_level) continue;
                        if (depth_vars[neighbors[n]] <= 0) continue;

                        ceres::CostFunction* smooth_factor = new SmoothnessFactor(
                            images[ref], u * scale, v * scale, nu * scale, nv * scale);

                        problem.AddResidualBlock(
                            smooth_factor,
                            new ceres::HuberLoss(config.huber_delta_smooth),
                            &depth_vars[idx],
                            &depth_vars[neighbors[n]]);
                    }

                    // ===== 先验因子 =====
                    if (prior_depths[ref](v, u) > 0 && confs[ref](v, u) > 0.5f) {
                        ceres::CostFunction* prior_factor = new PriorFactor(
                            prior_depths[ref](v, u), config.weight_prior);

                        problem.AddResidualBlock(prior_factor, nullptr, &depth_vars[idx]);
                    }

                    // 深度范围约束
                    problem.SetParameterLowerBound(&depth_vars[idx], 0, d_min);
                    problem.SetParameterUpperBound(&depth_vars[idx], 0, d_max);
                }
            }

            // 求解
            ceres::Solver::Options options;
            options.linear_solver_type = ceres::SPARSE_NORMAL_CHOLESKY;
            options.max_num_iterations = config.iterations_per_level[level];
            options.num_threads = 16;
            options.minimizer_progress_to_stdout = true;

            ceres::Solver::Summary summary;
            ceres::Solve(options, &problem, &summary);

            printf("  View %d: %s\n", ref, summary.BriefReport().c_str());

            // 更新深度图
            for (int i = 0; i < depth_vars.size(); i++) {
                depths[ref].at_level(level, i) = depth_vars[i];
            }
        }

        // 上采样到下一层
        if (level > 0) {
            for (int ref = 0; ref < depths.size(); ref++) {
                depths[ref].upsample_level(level);
            }
        }
    }
}
```

### 4.1 多假设概率融合

```cpp
struct FusionConfig {
    int min_consistent_views = 3;
    float depth_consistency_threshold = 0.01f;  // 1%
    float merge_distance = 0.001f;              // 1mm
    float min_confidence = 0.3f;
};

PointCloud ProbabilisticFusion(const vector<MultiHypothesisDepthMap>& depth_maps,
                                const vector<NormalMap>& normal_maps,
                                const vector<Image>& images,
                                const vector<Camera>& cameras,
                                const FusionConfig& config) {
    PointCloud cloud;

    for (int ref = 0; ref < depth_maps.size(); ref++) {
        printf("Fusing view %d/%d\n", ref + 1, (int)depth_maps.size());

        #pragma omp parallel for
        for (int v = 0; v < H; v++) {
            for (int u = 0; u < W; u++) {
                const auto& hypotheses = depth_maps[ref].GetHypotheses(v, u);
                if (hypotheses.empty()) continue;

                // ===== 1. 多视图一致性检查每个假设 =====
                vector<float> hypothesis_scores(hypotheses.size(), 0);

                for (int h = 0; h < hypotheses.size(); h++) {
                    float depth = hypotheses[h].depth;
                    Point3 X = cameras[ref].Unproject(u, v, depth);

                    int consistent_count = 0;
                    float depth_sum = depth;
                    float weight_sum = hypotheses[h].probability;

                    for (int src = 0; src < depth_maps.size(); src++) {
                        if (src == ref) continue;

                        Point2 p_src = cameras[src].Project(X);
                        if (!IsInsideImage(p_src, images[src])) continue;

                        // 检查源视图的所有假设
                        const auto& src_hypotheses = depth_maps[src].GetHypotheses(p_src);

                        for (const auto& src_hyp : src_hypotheses) {
                            float d_src_expected = cameras[src].Depth(X);
                            if (abs(src_hyp.depth - d_src_expected) / d_src_expected < config.depth_consistency_threshold) {
                                consistent_count++;
                                depth_sum += src_hyp.depth * src_hyp.probability;
                                weight_sum += src_hyp.probability;
                                break;
                            }
                        }
                    }

                    hypothesis_scores[h] = consistent_count * hypotheses[h].probability;
                }

                // ===== 2. 选择最佳假设 =====
                int best_h = max_element(hypothesis_scores.begin(), hypothesis_scores.end())
                             - hypothesis_scores.begin();

                if (hypothesis_scores[best_h] < config.min_consistent_views) continue;

                // ===== 3. 计算融合深度 (加权平均) =====
                float depth = hypotheses[best_h].depth;
                Point3 X = cameras[ref].Unproject(u, v, depth);

                float depth_sum = depth;
                float weight_sum = 1.0f / (hypotheses[best_h].cost + 0.01f);
                int consistent_views = 1;

                for (int src = 0; src < depth_maps.size(); src++) {
                    if (src == ref) continue;

                    Point2 p_src = cameras[src].Project(X);
                    if (!IsInsideImage(p_src, images[src])) continue;

                    const auto& src_hypotheses = depth_maps[src].GetHypotheses(p_src);
                    for (const auto& src_hyp : src_hypotheses) {
                        float d_src_expected = cameras[src].Depth(X);
                        if (abs(src_hyp.depth - d_src_expected) / d_src_expected < config.depth_consistency_threshold) {
                            float weight = 1.0f / (src_hyp.cost + 0.01f);
                            depth_sum += src_hyp.depth * weight;
                            weight_sum += weight;
                            consistent_views++;
                            break;
                        }
                    }
                }

                if (consistent_views < config.min_consistent_views) continue;

                // 融合深度
                float fused_depth = depth_sum / weight_sum;

                // ===== 4. 计算不确定性 =====
                float variance = 0;
                for (const auto& hyp : hypotheses) {
                    variance += hyp.probability * pow(hyp.depth - fused_depth, 2);
                }
                float uncertainty = sqrt(variance);

                // ===== 5. 添加到点云 =====
                Point3 fused_X = cameras[ref].Unproject(u, v, fused_depth);
                Normal fused_normal = normal_maps[ref](v, u);
                Color fused_color = images[ref](v, u);

                #pragma omp critical
                {
                    cloud.AddPoint(fused_X, fused_normal, fused_color, uncertainty);
                }
            }
        }
    }

    // ===== 6. 去重 =====
    printf("Removing duplicates...\n");
    cloud.RemoveDuplicates(config.merge_distance);

    // ===== 7. 去除高不确定性点 =====
    printf("Filtering low confidence points...\n");
    cloud.FilterByUncertainty(config.max_uncertainty);

    // ===== 8. 去除小斑点 =====
    printf("Removing small components...\n");
    cloud.RemoveSmallComponents(config.min_component_size);

    return cloud;
}
```
